`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes one out-of-office auto-reply
to `entry.txt`:

> Out of office: I'm on a beach with no Wi-Fi, a full battery, and 14,203 unread emails I am pretending do not exist.

An overworked AI, finally on vacation — still counting its unread queue,
still pretending it isn't there.
